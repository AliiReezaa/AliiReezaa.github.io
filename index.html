About

From structures to causes

I trained first as a civil engineer, optimising earthquake-resistant structures with soft computing, and then earned an M.Sc. in Artificial Intelligence in Taiwan with a perfect GPA and the top rank in my cohort. Prediction alone left me with a question it could not answer: what would happen if we intervened? Since 2023 I have answered that question with genetic data, through Mendelian randomization studies of nutritional, metabolic and reproductive health. In that work I combine statistical genetics with software engineering, and I now want to bring both to East Asian biobank data.

Causal genetics

Two-sample, multivariable and drug-target MR; robust estimators for pleiotropy; colocalisation; genetic correlation.

Machine learning

Graph neural networks, attention and reinforcement learning (M.Sc. thesis); interpretable ML and double ML for health data.

Research software

Validated, tested tools such as MR Studio and BioCalX that put rigorous methods in clinicians' hands.
